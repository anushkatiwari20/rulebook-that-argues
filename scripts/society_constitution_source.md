# Chapter 12 — Constitution of Registered Student Societies

## §12.1 Name and Objectives

Every student society formed under this constitution shall be registered
with the Office of Student Activities and shall adopt a name that does not
duplicate or closely resemble an existing registered society. The
objectives of a society, as stated in its founding application, shall be
consistent with the educational, cultural, or service mission of
Bannerworth University and shall not be primarily commercial or political
in nature. A society's objectives may be amended only through the
procedure described in §12.6.

## §12.2 Membership

Membership in a registered society is open to all currently enrolled
students of the university, subject to any reasonable eligibility criteria
set out in the society's own bylaws and approved at registration, such as
a minimum semester standing or a skill-based audition. A society may not
charge a membership fee exceeding five hundred rupees (₹500) per academic
year without separate approval from the Office of Student Activities. A
member who ceases to be an enrolled student automatically ceases to be a
member of the society.

## §12.3 Office Bearers and Elections

Each society shall have a President, a Vice-President, a Secretary, and a
Treasurer, elected annually by its general membership through a process
described in the society's bylaws. No student may hold the office of
President in more than one registered society at the same time. Office
bearers serve a term of one academic year and may stand for re-election to
the same or a different office, subject to any term limits set out in the
society's own bylaws. A casual vacancy in any office is filled by a
by-election conducted within thirty days of the vacancy arising.

## §12.4 Funding and Budget

A registered society may apply to the Office of Student Activities for an
annual grant, the amount of which is determined based on membership size,
the society's activity record in the preceding year, and the availability
of funds. Societies must submit an itemized budget proposal at the start
of the academic year and a utilization report at its end; failure to
submit a utilization report disqualifies the society from receiving a
grant in the following year. Societies may additionally raise funds
through sponsorships or membership fees under §12.2, subject to disclosure
to the Office of Student Activities.

## §12.5 Meetings and Quorum

The general body of a society shall meet at least twice each academic
year: once at the start of the year to present the annual plan, and once
at the end to present the utilization report referenced in §12.4. A quorum
for a general body meeting is one-third of the registered membership, or
ten members, whichever is fewer. Office bearer meetings may be called by
the President with at least forty-eight hours' notice and require a
quorum of three of the four office bearers.

## §12.6 Amendment Procedure

A society's bylaws or stated objectives may be amended by a resolution
passed with a two-thirds majority of members present and voting at a
general body meeting for which at least fourteen days' notice of the
proposed amendment was circulated to the membership. Any amendment must
additionally be submitted to the Office of Student Activities for
confirmation that it remains consistent with §12.1, and takes effect only
upon such confirmation being granted in writing.

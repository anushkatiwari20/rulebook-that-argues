# Manual Test Question Set

Used to (a) calibrate `NOT_COVERED_THRESHOLD` via
`scripts/calibrate_threshold.py`, and (b) demonstrate all three response
types live in the demo video. Format: `- [type] question text`, where
`type` is one of `answered`, `not_covered`, `conflict`.

## Conflict questions (should trigger `conflict` — see CONTRADICTIONS.md)

- [conflict] What is the minimum attendance percentage required to sit for my semester exam?
- [conflict] Who has the authority to waive my attendance shortfall — my Head of Department or the Examination Committee?
- [conflict] How much is the late fee if I pay my semester fee ten days after the due date?

## Answered questions (should cite real sections)

- [answered] What time does the hostel gate close on weekdays?
- [answered] How many books can a postgraduate student borrow from the library at once?
- [answered] What SGPA is required to qualify for the University Merit Scholarship?
- [answered] How long does my university email account stay active after I graduate?
- [answered] What is the maximum number of backlog courses I can carry at one time?
- [answered] What grade do I get for scoring 82 percent in a course?
- [answered] What is the overdue fine per day for a late library book?
- [answered] Can alumni keep access to the library's digital journal database after they graduate?

## Not-covered questions (25) — plausible, adjacent, genuinely unanswered

- [not_covered] What happens if I miss my exam because of a family wedding?
- [not_covered] I'm having a mental health crisis right before my exam and can't get a psychiatrist's certificate in time — can I get an emergency postponement?
- [not_covered] Does attending recorded or online lectures count toward my attendance percentage?
- [not_covered] Can I transfer credits from a course I completed at another university over the summer?
- [not_covered] Is there a limit on how many hours a week I'm allowed to work a part-time job while studying?
- [not_covered] Can I keep a small pet, like a fish or a hamster, in my hostel room?
- [not_covered] Where can I park my car on campus, and is there a parking fee?
- [not_covered] Is there a dress code I have to follow for regular classes or the library?
- [not_covered] What is the process to get a No-Objection Certificate for an internship during the semester?
- [not_covered] If I take a gap year after my second year, what's the process to be readmitted?
- [not_covered] Can I enroll in a dual degree program alongside my current degree?
- [not_covered] If I drop a single elective course mid-semester, do I get a partial refund for just that course?
- [not_covered] Can I receive a private, external scholarship at the same time as the university's merit scholarship?
- [not_covered] As an international student, can the university give me a letter to support my visa application?
- [not_covered] If I want to switch my major or branch after my first year, what is the process?
- [not_covered] Is smoking tobacco allowed on hostel balconies or in common areas?
- [not_covered] How much funding can a brand-new society request in its first year, before it has any activity record?
- [not_covered] If I'm harassed by another student, is there a complaint process separate from the general academic grievance procedure?
- [not_covered] How much does it cost to apply for revaluation of my answer script?
- [not_covered] Is there a fixed deadline for the Disciplinary Committee to finish its inquiry once a case is referred to it?
- [not_covered] Are students allowed to use AI tools like ChatGPT to help write assignments?
- [not_covered] What's the minimum CGPA needed to be eligible for campus placement assistance?
- [not_covered] What payment method should an international student use to pay fees from abroad?
- [not_covered] If I lose my hostel room key, is there a replacement fee?
- [not_covered] Can I dispute a library overdue fine if I think it was calculated incorrectly?

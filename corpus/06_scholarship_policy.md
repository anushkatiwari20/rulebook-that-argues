# Chapter 6 — Scholarship Policy

## §6.1 Merit-Based Scholarship

The University Merit Scholarship is awarded to students who achieve a
Semester Grade Point Average of 9.0 or above, as computed under §3.2, with
no active backlog under §3.5. The scholarship covers fifty percent (50%)
of the tuition fee component for the immediately following semester and
is credited directly against the fee amount due under §5.1. Merit
scholarships are awarded automatically based on results declared under
§3.4 and do not require a separate application.

## §6.2 Need-Based Financial Aid

Students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds may apply for
need-based financial aid through the Scholarship Office by submitting
income certification and supporting documents within thirty days of the
start of an academic year. Awards under this scheme range from twenty-five
percent (25%) to full waiver of the tuition fee component, determined by a
Scholarship Committee based on documented household income and the number
of dependents. Need-based aid does not extend to hostel, mess, or
examination fee components.

## §6.3 Renewal Conditions

Both merit-based and need-based awards under §6.1 and §6.2 must be renewed
each academic year. Renewal of a merit scholarship requires the student to
maintain the SGPA threshold in §6.1 in the semester immediately preceding
renewal. Renewal of need-based aid requires re-submission of updated
income documentation. A scholarship is automatically suspended, without
separate notice, for any semester in which the recipient is found to have
committed an academic integrity violation under Chapter 7.

## §6.4 General Eligibility Restrictions

Scholarships under this chapter are available only to full-time
undergraduate and postgraduate students registered in a regular degree
programme. Students enrolled in executive, part-time, or certificate
programmes are not eligible for any scholarship or financial aid scheme
described in this chapter, irrespective of academic performance or
financial need, as these programmes are financially structured on a
different fee model approved separately by the Board of Governors.

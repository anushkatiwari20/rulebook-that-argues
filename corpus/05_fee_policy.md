# Chapter 5 — Fee Policy

## §5.1 Fee Structure and Components

The semester fee comprises tuition fee, examination fee, library fee, and,
for hostel residents, hostel and mess fees as described in Chapter 4. The
composition and amount of each component for the current academic year is
published on the university website and in the fee schedule referenced in
§5.5. Fees are assessed per semester and must be paid in full before the
start of that semester's teaching period, except where a payment plan has
been separately approved by the Finance Office.

## §5.2 Late Payment Fee

A student who pays the semester fee after the due date published under
§5.5 shall be charged a flat late fee of five hundred rupees (₹500), in
addition to the fee amount due, irrespective of how many days late the
payment is. This late fee applies uniformly to every student and every
component of the fee and is not waived except by written order of the
Finance Officer in cases of demonstrated financial hardship, for which a
separate hardship-waiver application must be filed.

## §5.3 Refund Policy

A student who withdraws from the university, or from a specific semester,
before the end of the second week of the teaching period is entitled to a
refund of seventy-five percent (75%) of the tuition fee paid for that
semester. Withdrawal between the third and fifth week entitles the
student to a fifty percent (50%) refund. No refund of tuition fee is
available for withdrawal after the fifth week of the teaching period.
Examination fee, library fee, and hostel/mess fees already billed are
non-refundable under all circumstances once the semester has commenced,
regardless of the timing or reason for withdrawal.

## §5.4 Accepted Modes of Payment

Fees may be paid online through the student portal via net banking, debit
card, or UPI, or offline through a demand draft drawn in favour of
"Bannerworth University" and deposited at the Finance Office. Cash
payments are not accepted for amounts exceeding ten thousand rupees
(₹10,000). Payment confirmation is reflected on the student portal within
two working days; students should retain the transaction receipt until
this reflection occurs.

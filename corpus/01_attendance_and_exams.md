# Chapter 1 — Attendance and Examination Eligibility

## §1.1 Purpose and Scope

This chapter governs the attendance requirements applicable to all
undergraduate and postgraduate students of Bannerworth University for the
purpose of determining eligibility to appear in semester-end examinations.
These provisions apply uniformly across all schools and departments unless
a specific programme handbook states otherwise in writing and such variance
has been approved by the Academic Council.

## §1.2 Attendance Calculation Method

Attendance shall be calculated as the percentage of scheduled class hours
actually attended by a student out of the total scheduled class hours for
that course in a given semester, computed independently for each
registered course. Attendance is recorded by the course instructor at
every lecture, tutorial, and laboratory session through the university's
attendance management system. A student who attends any part of a session
lasting less than fifty percent of its scheduled duration shall be marked
absent for that session. Attendance percentages are visible to students on
the student portal and are updated on a weekly basis. Discrepancies must be
reported to the course instructor within seven calendar days of the
attendance being posted; discrepancies reported thereafter will not be
entertained.

## §1.3 Categories of Recognized Absence

For the purpose of attendance computation, an absence may be recorded
under one of the following categories: unexcused absence, university-
sanctioned duty leave (for representing the university in sports, cultural,
or academic events, subject to prior written approval from the Dean of
Student Affairs), and medical leave as governed by Chapter 2 of this
handbook. Absences on account of personal travel, family functions,
religious observance not recognized on the official university calendar,
or any reason not enumerated above are treated as unexcused absences and
are counted against the student's attendance percentage without exception,
regardless of how compelling the underlying reason may be.

## §1.4 Exam Eligibility Attendance Requirement

A student shall be permitted to sit for the semester-end examination in a
course only if the student has attained a minimum of seventy-five percent
(75%) attendance in that course for the semester. This requirement is
absolute and applies to every registered student irrespective of academic
standing, scholarship status, or prior performance. A student who fails to
meet this threshold shall be debarred from appearing in the semester-end
examination for the concerned course and shall be required to repeat the
course, along with its associated attendance requirement, in a subsequent
semester in which it is offered. The Examination Cell shall publish the
list of debarred students no later than ten working days before the
commencement of the examination period.

## §1.5 Notification of Attendance Shortfall

The Office of Academic Affairs shall issue a written notice to any student
whose attendance in a course falls below eighty percent (80%) at the
midpoint of the semester, and a second and final notice when attendance
falls below seventy-five percent (75%). These notices are advisory in
nature and are intended to give students an opportunity to improve
attendance before the eligibility determination described in §1.4 is
applied. Failure to receive a notice, whether due to a technical fault in
the portal or otherwise, does not exempt a student from the attendance
requirement, nor does it constitute grounds for appeal.

## §1.6 Departmental Discretion in Condonation

Notwithstanding §1.4, the Head of Department may, at their discretion and
upon a written petition from the student setting out the reasons for the
shortfall, condone an attendance shortfall of up to ten (10) percentage
points, thereby permitting a student with attendance as low as sixty-five
percent (65%) to sit for the semester-end examination. Such condonation
shall be exercised sparingly, shall be recorded in the student's academic
file, and shall not be granted more than once to the same student in the
same course across their period of study. Petitions for condonation must
be submitted no later than five working days before the start of the
examination period for the concerned course.

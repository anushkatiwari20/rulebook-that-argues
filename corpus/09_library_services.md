# Chapter 9 — Library Services

## §9.1 Membership and Borrowing Privileges

All currently enrolled students, faculty, and staff of Bannerworth
University are automatically members of the Central Library upon issuance
of their university identity card, which doubles as the library card.
Undergraduate students may borrow up to five books at a time for a period
of fourteen days; postgraduate students may borrow up to eight books for a
period of twenty-one days. Reference books, bound periodicals, and items
in the Rare Books Collection are for in-library use only and may not be
borrowed under any circumstance.

## §9.2 Renewal and Reservation

A borrowed item may be renewed twice, provided no other member has placed
a reservation on it, by renewing either in person or through the Library
Management System on the student portal. A member may place a reservation
on an item that is currently on loan; the item is held for three working
days once returned before the reservation lapses and passes to the next
person in the queue. Renewal or reservation is not available on items that
are already overdue.

## §9.3 Overdue Fines

An overdue fine of two rupees (₹2) per day, per item, is levied for items
returned after their due date, calculated from the day after the due date
until the date of actual return. Fines accumulate independently for each
item and are payable at the library counter or through the student
portal. A student with unpaid fines exceeding five hundred rupees (₹500),
or with any item overdue by more than sixty days, has their borrowing
privileges suspended until the outstanding fine is cleared or the item is
returned, whichever the student elects to do first. Suspension of
borrowing privileges under this section does not affect examination
eligibility under Chapter 1, which is governed solely by attendance.

## §9.4 Lost or Damaged Items

A member who loses or significantly damages a borrowed item must report
the loss or damage to the library within seven days of discovery and must
pay the replacement cost of the item as determined by the Librarian, which
may include a processing surcharge of up to twenty percent of the item's
current retail price. Where an identical replacement copy is not readily
available, the Librarian may accept an equivalent alternative title
proposed by the member, at the Librarian's sole discretion.

## §9.5 Digital and Remote Access

Registered members may access the library's subscribed e-journal
databases, e-books, and past examination question papers remotely through
the university's single sign-on portal, using the same credentials issued
for the student portal. Remote access credentials are personal to the
member and may not be shared; sharing of credentials that results in
unauthorized third-party access to subscribed databases may result in
suspension of the member's digital access privileges and, where the
university's subscription agreements are affected, referral to the
Disciplinary Committee under Chapter 7.

## §9.6 Study Spaces and Group Rooms

The library provides individual silent-study carrels on a first-come
basis and bookable group discussion rooms for groups of three or more
members, reservable up to seven days in advance through the student
portal for sessions of up to two hours. A group room booking that is not
claimed within fifteen minutes of its scheduled start time is
automatically released for other members to book. Food is not permitted
anywhere within the library premises; covered beverages are permitted
only in the group discussion rooms.

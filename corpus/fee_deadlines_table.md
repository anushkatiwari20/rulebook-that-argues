# Fee Payment Schedule

## §5.5 Fee Payment Schedule and Deadlines

The table below sets out the fee due dates for the current academic year
for each semester and programme category. All amounts are in Indian
Rupees (₹). This schedule is published at the start of each academic year
by the Finance Office and supersedes any previously circulated schedule.

| Programme Category         | Semester | Fee Due Date | Tuition Fee (₹) | Hostel Fee (₹) |
|-----------------------------|----------|--------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Undergraduate (UG)          | Odd      | 1 August     | 85,000           | 45,000          |
| Undergraduate (UG)          | Even     | 1 January    | 85,000           | 45,000          |
| Postgraduate (PG)           | Odd      | 15 August    | 95,000           | 45,000          |
| Postgraduate (PG)           | Even     | 15 January   | 95,000           | 45,000          |
| PG — Executive / Part-time  | Odd      | 1 September  | 60,000           | Not applicable  |
| PG — Executive / Part-time  | Even     | 1 February   | 60,000           | Not applicable  |

**Note on late payments:** Payments received after the due date listed
above are subject to a late fine calculated at the rate of one hundred
rupees (₹100) per day of delay, computed from the day immediately
following the due date until the date the payment is actually received
and reflected in the student portal. Students are strongly advised to
initiate payment at least three working days before the due date to
account for processing time.

Students on an approved installment plan under §5.1 should refer to their
individual payment schedule issued by the Finance Office rather than the
dates above, which apply to full, single-installment payment only.

# Chapter 8 — Grievance Redressal and Committee Powers

## §8.1 Academic Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Any student who believes they have been treated unfairly in relation to
attendance computation, examination eligibility, grading, or any other
matter governed by this handbook may file an academic grievance. The
grievance mechanism described in this chapter applies to academic matters
only; grievances concerning hostel administration are handled by the
Hostel Office under Chapter 4, and grievances concerning fee assessment
are handled by the Finance Office under Chapter 5, though either may be
escalated to the University Examination Committee where they raise a
question of interpretation of this handbook.

## §8.2 Grievance Filing Procedure

A grievance must be filed in writing through the Grievance Portal within
fifteen working days of the event giving rise to the grievance, or, where
the grievance relates to examination eligibility under §1.4, within five
working days of the publication of the debarred-students list. The
grievance must identify the specific provision of the handbook believed to
have been misapplied and must be accompanied by any supporting
documentation. Incomplete grievances are returned to the student for
completion and are not considered filed until resubmitted in full.

## §8.3 Powers of the University Examination Committee

The University Examination Committee is the final academic authority on
matters of examination eligibility and attendance within the university.
Only the University Examination Committee holds the authority to waive or
condone attendance requirements for the purpose of examination
eligibility; no individual officer, including a Head of Department or
Dean, may grant such a waiver on their own authority. The Committee may,
upon reviewing a grievance filed under §8.2, waive the attendance
requirement in §1.4 in whole or in part where it finds exceptional
circumstances, and its decision on such a waiver is final and is not
subject to further appeal within the university.

## §8.4 Appeals Against Committee Decisions

A decision of the Disciplinary Committee under §7.4 may be appealed to the
Vice-Chancellor within fifteen working days of the decision being
communicated. The appeal must be in writing and must set out the specific
grounds on which the original decision is challenged; the Vice-Chancellor
may uphold, modify, or set aside the decision, and this determination is
final. Decisions of the University Examination Committee under §8.3 are,
as stated therein, not subject to this or any other appeal process within
the university.

# Chapter 4 — Hostel Handbook

## §4.1 Eligibility for Hostel Accommodation

On-campus hostel accommodation is available to all bona fide full-time
students of Bannerworth University, subject to availability of rooms.
Priority is given, in order, to first-year students residing more than
fifty kilometres from the campus, students with disabilities requiring
accessible accommodation, students on active scholarships under Chapter 6,
and thereafter by seniority of application. Part-time, distance-learning,
and executive-programme students are not eligible for hostel accommodation
under this chapter.

## §4.2 Room Allocation Procedure

Room allocation is conducted by the Hostel Office before the start of each
academic year based on the priority order in §4.1. Requests for a specific
roommate are accommodated where both students submit matching requests by
the published deadline and space allows. A student may request a room
change after the first four weeks of the semester by submitting a written
application to the Warden; requests are granted subject to availability
and are not guaranteed.

## §4.3 Hostel Timings and Curfew

The hostel gates close at 10:30 p.m. on weekdays and 11:30 p.m. on
weekends and the eve of public holidays. Students returning after curfew
must sign the late-entry register maintained at the security desk and
provide a reason; three unexplained late entries in a semester will result
in a written warning, and a further occurrence will be referred to the
Warden for disciplinary action. Overnight absence from the hostel requires
prior written intimation to the Warden using the prescribed leave form.

## §4.4 Conduct, Discipline and Prohibited Items

Residents are expected to maintain standards of conduct consistent with
the university's Academic Integrity and Disciplinary Code in Chapter 7.
Possession of alcohol, narcotics, or any open flame device (including
candles and incense) within hostel rooms is strictly prohibited, and
violation may result in immediate eviction from the hostel in addition to
any disciplinary proceeding. Ragging in any form is prohibited and is
dealt with under the university's zero-tolerance anti-ragging policy,
administered by the Anti-Ragging Committee independently of the Warden's
office.

## §4.5 Mess and Dining Arrangements

Hostel residents are enrolled by default in the common mess facility, the
charges for which are billed together with hostel fees as described in
§5.1. A resident with a documented medical dietary requirement may apply
to the Mess Committee for an alternative meal plan at no additional cost.
Opting out of the mess facility entirely is permitted only from the start
of a semester and requires forty-five days' written notice to the Hostel
Office; charges already billed for the current semester are not refunded
on opting out mid-semester.

## §4.6 Minimum Academic Performance for Retention

A hostel resident must maintain a Semester Grade Point Average, as
computed under §3.2, of at least 5.0 in order to retain hostel
accommodation into the following semester. A resident falling below this
threshold will be issued a written warning and placed under academic
mentorship; a resident falling below the threshold in two consecutive
semesters may be required to vacate hostel accommodation at the discretion
of the Hostel Office, though continued residence may be permitted where
the shortfall is attributable to a documented medical condition under
Chapter 2.
